About

I'm Raelyn Zhang (张芷睿), currently based in Sweden. I hold an MA in Asian Studies from Stockholm University and spent a semester on exchange at Chuo University in Tokyo.

My work sits at the intersection of AI-driven content production and creative automation. At Stellar Music Group in Copenhagen, I lead image and video production using Midjourney and ComfyUI, build n8n automation workflows for scheduled content generation, and manage multi-platform social strategy. Previously at Gaddr in Stockholm, I built reusable ComfyUI pipelines and Stable Diffusion workflows to accelerate creative output across international teams.

Outside of work, I run multiple TikTok accounts and write my own Python tools — including a full video production dashboard and a beat-synced auto-editor — because I'd rather automate the tedious parts and focus on what's actually interesting.
